# Staff guide

Open the marketing page on the office computer. It shows Content, Approvals, Enquiries, Reviews, Visibility, and Performance.

To approve a post, type `Y` and the post number, then record the reply. Type `N` to reject it. Type the replacement text to ask for a change. The page does not send the post.

Enquiries stay held until Cindy approves the draft in her WhatsApp chat. A complaint stays escalated and has no public reply.

Reviews are drafts. They are not sent. Visibility checks compare a field with what you observed.

Performance shows recorded figures. A blank figure stays zero. The monthly report uses only those figures. The last line is blank for what to test next.

Choose WRITE before saving a review or a visibility check. READ can look but cannot save.
